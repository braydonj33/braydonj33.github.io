      About this map

      Lines show children identified as taken from Ukraine to Russia, and documented onward transfers from Russia to Belarus and North Korea. Bars show documented facilities in each country, split by type. Each country is one point, not a camp location. Counts are minimums, and counts below 5 are shown as "fewer than 5".

      Sources: Government of Ukraine (2026); Humanitarian Research Lab at Yale School of Public Health (2023b, 2025a, 2025b); "Thousands of Ukrainian children taken to Belarus" (2023); Vakulina (2025). Facility types classified by the authors.
